Profile

About

I am an electrical and energy-data engineer with experience spanning EHV power-system operations, electrical measurements, energy-meter testing, data analysis and laboratory automation.

I currently work as an Academic Research Associate at the Institute for Sustainable Energy Systems (INES), Hochschule Offenburg. My work focuses on data-driven assessment of electricity meters, long-term analysis of meter-test data and development of automated electrical test systems.

My current activities combine large SQL-based measurement datasets with Python analytics and hands-on laboratory integration. I develop data-processing and KPI workflows, investigate long-term measurement behaviour, and integrate programmable power equipment, reference measurement devices, energy meters and databases using Node-RED, Modbus, RS-485 and GPIB/VISA.

Before moving to Germany, I worked in EHV substation operations at Kerala State Electricity Board. I hold an M.Sc. in Renewable Energy and Data Engineering from Hochschule Offenburg and a B.Tech. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from the University of Kerala.
