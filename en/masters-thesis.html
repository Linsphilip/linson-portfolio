Master’s Thesis — Digital Twin

Measurement-based modelling of a passive electrical efficiency filter to investigate its operating behaviour and energy-saving potential.

Organization

Livarsa GmbH · Hochschule Offenburg

Focus

MATLAB / Simscape / LTspice / Parameter identification

My contribution

  • Developed and validated a digital twin of an industrial electrical efficiency filter system.
  • Performed electrical measurements and measurement-based parameter identification.
  • Modelled the system using LTspice and MATLAB/Simscape and compared simulations with real measurements.
  • Results published in the IEEE Open Access Journal of Power and Energy (2026).
